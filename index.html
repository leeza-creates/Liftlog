Support & next steps

When should someone reach out?

It can be useful to talk with a trusted adult, school counsellor, doctor, or qualified mental health professional when changes in mood, thoughts, behaviour, sleep, concentration, or daily functioning persist, become difficult to manage, or interfere with everyday life.

  • You do not need to be certain about a diagnosis before asking for support.
  • Sharing what you have noticed can be a useful starting point.
  • Support can come from trusted people as well as qualified professionals.
